# Code of Conduct

Help keep this project useful and welcoming. Treat people with respect, explain your reasoning, and critique the work rather than the person. Different experience levels, backgrounds, and accessibility needs are welcome.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and repeated personal attacks are not acceptable. Do not post another person's private information or contact them repeatedly after being asked to stop.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close a discussion, warn a participant, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. They should explain decisions where practical and handle reports with care. This policy applies in project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces.

To report a concern, contact a maintainer through a private contact method listed on their GitHub profile. If none is available, open an issue requesting a private conduct-reporting channel without naming affected people or describing sensitive details publicly. Reports involving a maintainer should go to another maintainer when one is available.

Good-faith disagreement, a rejected proposal, and a request for evidence are normal parts of collaboration. Keep the discussion focused, specific, and open to correction.
